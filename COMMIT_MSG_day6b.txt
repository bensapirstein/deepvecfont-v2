Read the paper: three protocol deviations, and rebuild Job C around coverage

DeepVecFontV2.pdf lands in the repo root. The first five days of this project
ran on 1.3's transcribed numbers alone; reading Sec. 4.1 against our own
protocol turns up three deviations, now recorded in 1.4.

  - Ns is 10 on English in the paper, 50 on Chinese. Every English row in
    RESULTS.csv used 50, so our English figures are optimistic against the
    paper's own protocol and the 0.0584-vs-0.052 comparison in 2.4 is not
    like-for-like. Note the direction, it is counter-intuitive: this
    STRENGTHENS 2.4, whose argument rests on English nearly reproducing while
    Chinese does not. Paired Stage 2 deltas are untouched because both arms
    used 50, so N cancels the way the raster-convention floor does.
  - The Chinese training set is meant to be augmented 10x by affine transform.
    data_utils/augment.py implements exactly this, defaults to --n_aug 5, and
    is an offline build step; whether our Chinese data was built with it is
    nowhere in the record. That is a live lead on the open ~0.037 Chinese
    residual, and it costs no GPU to check.
  - Test split counts: the paper says 1425 English fonts, 0 records 1,386.

Two things confirmed matching so they are not re-litigated: candidate selection
is by highest IoU in both the paper and test_few_shot.py, and the optimizer,
learning rate, resolution and reference-glyph counts all agree with COMMANDS.md.
Also for 6: Tab. 3's sampling-point study has individual steps of 0.0002 to
0.0014, so like Tab. 1's ablation every row in it sits below our 0.0038 English
floor. Two of the paper's own tables, not one, whose individual rows this
instrument could not have resolved.

Job C is rebuilt. It was three more English baseline seeds; it is now one
replicated candidate per category from the assignment's own example list, which
is what the required paper-vs-reconstruction-vs-improved table needs. Six
representatives (E2 batchnorm, E4 ngf32, E5 bneck256, E7 aux01, E11 adamw,
E3 refine2), three seeds each on Chinese, ~18 GPU-h, all trained with
--max_ckpt_keep 10 and scored at matched epoch 150 so the epoch confound Job A
found never enters the table. Screening is not repeated: the 26 rows already in
RESULTS.csv are the screen, and re-picking a leader at one seed is the measured
1-in-3 path. E7 is preferred over E12's better number because val_metric
excludes the KL term, so E12's own selection is blind to what E12 varies.

Job C-EN runs the same six at one seed on English, ~105 GPU-h. Screening only,
pre-committed as such, and it exists because the instructor requires coverage of
both datasets as the original paper reports them. Scored at the paper's Ns of 10
with the seed-1111 anchor rescored to match, which also measures for free how
much n_samples 50 flattered every existing English row.

The three extra English baseline seeds are demoted to optional, with the
reasoning kept because it generalizes: they cannot change a conclusion. 5.2 is
mixed sign and mixed sign is floor-independent. The two 6 claims they prop up
move in opposite directions, and since the range of a sample grows with n, six
seeds most likely widen the spread and weaken the one that currently clears.
eval_noise.sh against English is the cheap substitute if 6 turns out to need it.

Runbook renumbered to eleven sections for the six jobs. Nothing here moves the
critical path, which is still REPORT.md 4, 5 and 6.
